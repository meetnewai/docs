# MeetNewAI changelog

User-facing changes to [meetnewai.com](https://meetnewai.com). Dates are release dates (UTC+8). Internal and infrastructure changes are left out. On-site version: <https://meetnewai.com/changelog> (RSS: <https://meetnewai.com/rss.xml>).

<!-- changelog:start -->
## 2026-10-18
- **AI models section**: model pages with benchmark snapshots, Codex Radar coding-agent runs and real subscription price per million tokens, each source named on the page. A plan finder on model pages.
- **Directory grows to 406 listings**: AI products, everyday tools, MCP servers and agent skills, each checked on its official site, with tags, alternatives, comparisons and use-case pages. Later the same day: 36 more verified entries and use-case hubs, 15 entries from WorthToTry leads (CC BY 4.0, re-checked), 4 more, then 18 AI tools and 7 browser AI games.
- **Sign in and submit a tool**: submissions go through a moderation queue; approved submissions are published to the directory.
- **API cost calculator** at [/models/api-cost-calculator](https://meetnewai.com/models/api-cost-calculator).
- **API pricing** at [/models/pricing](https://meetnewai.com/models/pricing) with 10 vendor pages; API prices on model and compare pages; a [/free](https://meetnewai.com/free) hub.
- **Traffic growth board** at [/boards/traffic-growth](https://meetnewai.com/boards/traffic-growth), using Vaneform monthly visit estimates, and a traffic line on product pages.
- **Listings feed** at [/api/tools.json](https://meetnewai.com/api/tools.json) (weekly copy in [meetnewai/data](https://github.com/meetnewai/data)).
- **MCP install snippets** for 35 servers, taken from official READMEs and docs; a maker badge at [/badge](https://meetnewai.com/badge).
- New listings show the date they were added.
- Privacy policy, terms and an RSS feed of the changelog.
- Accessibility pass (keyboard-friendly search palette, AA contrast, focusable tables); search results open at /discover; bilingual ranked search with jumps to hubs; 48-card paging and an A–Z list.
- Visual polish: official logos and monograms, category icons, tidier chips and tiles, better phone layouts; 1200×630 social cards in English and Chinese.

## 2026-10-17
- **Higgsfield joins the featured picks** on the home page.
- **19 more games play inside the page**, including INKWAVE, Krunker, Shell Shockers, Quake, HexGL, WorldGuessr and Orb.Farm.
- **Brand and press kit** at [/brand](https://meetnewai.com/brand); the About page credits the maker and sibling sites.

## 2026-10-16
- **AI games play inside the page**: game pages open a 16:9 play frame (12 games at launch, including Little Prince, StoryComet, Penguin Smack, DRIFT, Infinite Worlds and AI Dungeon).
- Three utilities: account format conversion, the GPT IQ index and the Codex reset radar.
- Four notes: [What is MCP](articles/what-is-mcp.md), [AI coding assistant](articles/ai-coding-tools.md), [AI games online](articles/ai-games-online.md) and [AI tools for presentations](articles/ai-ppt.md).
- The free AI API page lists 29 offers you can apply for, filtered by type, region and gate.
- Unknown pages return a real 404; structured data, social tags and llms.txt added.

## 2026-10-15
- **The directory opens by job**: categories list uses by field; AI tools for coding live under their own category.
- The home page is search, featured tools, then the full directory.
- The free AI API page is in the navigation.
