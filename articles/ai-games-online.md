# AI games online

Originally published at <https://meetnewai.com/blog/ai-games-online> on 2026-10-16 by Kai · 中文版: <https://meetnewai.com/zh/blog/ai-games-online>

## Summary

AI games online, in MeetNewAI's sense, are the ones that start right in the browser. MeetNewAI's AI game pages put a play frame on the detail page, with the author and source on the same page. The games in this note were play-tested between October 13 and October 15, 2026.

The note sorts them into two ways in. If you want a story: The Little Prince: Journey of the Stars (a free 3D starfield by Sac), StoryComet (a moving picture book by Meng To, first three books free), Infinite Worlds (the story changes with the choices you write, with sessions of about 12 minutes) and AI Dungeon (an adventure grown from one setup line, with free and paid plans). If you want controls: Penguin Smack (free 3D arcade by GamesByAI), Dustlight (lamp-carrying across night-sky islands, MIT licensed, also by GamesByAI) and Bearzerk! (auto-firing weapons, free). Before you press play, check that the page has a play frame, how long a session runs, and whether the author, source and price are written down.

## Key points and data

- Play tests ran October 13–15, 2026.
- Story picks: The Little Prince: Journey of the Stars, StoryComet, Infinite Worlds, AI Dungeon.
- Control picks: Penguin Smack, Dustlight (MIT), Bearzerk!.
- Infinite Worlds sessions run about 12 minutes; StoryComet's first three books are free.
- Three checks: play frame, session length, and author/source/price.
