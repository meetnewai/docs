Originally published at <https://meetnewai.com/blog/ai-ppt> on 2026-10-16 by Kai · 中文版: <https://meetnewai.com/zh/blog/ai-ppt>

## Summary

AI tools for presentations can turn one sentence into a deck draft you can discuss. The first version handles structure and page order, but the numbers, examples and judgment still have to come from you. This note uses Gamma as the example: it turns one idea into a presentation, a document or a web page, which helps when you want to see the visual structure, keep editing and then share. MeetNewAI's record lists a free plan and paid plans, with quotas following gamma.app, last checked October 13, 2026.

The note's method is practical. Before the first prompt, write down the audience, the decision they should make or the one thing they should remember, and roughly how many pages. An example is a ten-minute talk for product colleagues, six pages, ending with a page that asks them to confirm the schedule. After the draft appears, rewrite each title so it states that page's conclusion, delete repeated pages, replace placeholder numbers, names and dates with your own, and re-lay out the result in a company template if needed.

## Key points and data

- Example tool: Gamma, with free and paid plans recorded (checked October 13, 2026).
- Put audience, desired decision and page count into the first request.
- Make every slide title state its conclusion; keep the more specific of two duplicate pages.
- Replace all placeholder numbers, names and dates with your own.
